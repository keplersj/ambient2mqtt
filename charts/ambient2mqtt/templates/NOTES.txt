ambient2mqtt is installed as {{ include "ambient2mqtt.fullname" . }}.

It publishes to MQTT at {{ .Values.mqtt.host }}:{{ .Values.mqtt.port }} and emits Home
Assistant discovery under the "{{ .Values.bridge.discoveryPrefix }}/" prefix. Make sure

{{- end }}
